Mobile notification channel

How phone notifications (entry pass, meal stubs, meeting links and updates, kit-claim, edit-link and broadcast messages) are delivered to registrants who chose a phone channel. Email delivery is unaffected.

chat Viber sms SMS

Viber sends the QR entry pass as a picture; registrants need the Viber app. SMS is text only: it may include a link to open the QR entry pass, and registrants can always use the QR sent by email or check in by face.
